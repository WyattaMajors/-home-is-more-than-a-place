01 / WHY THIS PROJECT

One issue.
A lot of people.

Homelessness is often talked about as a statistic. This project is about remembering the people behind those numbers.

I started this project because I wanted to understand homelessness beyond headlines and learn more about what people in my own community experience. Through volunteering with Helpline House, I have had an opportunity to see how important community support can be.

Project goal: Learn, share reliable information, connect people with legitimate community resources, and encourage meaningful action.
