Why I'm doing this

I wanted to understand more than the statistics.

Homelessness is something I wanted to learn about beyond numbers and headlines. I wanted to understand some of the different reasons people can experience homelessness and hear from people who actually work with the issue.

This project is my way of learning, asking questions, and sharing what I find with other people. I also wanted to make the project measurable so I can see how it grows over time.

About the project

Learning first. Acting second.

The goal isn't simply to talk about homelessness. The project is designed to learn about its causes, understand different perspectives, and find ways to make the work measurable.

01

Research homelessness using reliable sources and learn about the different factors that can lead to housing instability.
