Why I'm doing this

I wanted to understand the issue instead of just hearing about it.

Homelessness is something I have heard about many times, but I wanted to learn more about what actually causes it and what people experiencing it go through.

Instead of making assumptions, I wanted to research reliable information, listen to people who work directly with homelessness, and learn from people with firsthand experience.

This project is my way of taking what I learn and sharing it with other people while keeping track of how the project actually reaches the community.

About the project

Learning first. Acting second.

The goal isn't simply to talk about homelessness. The project is designed to learn about its causes, understand different perspectives, and find ways to make the work measurable.
