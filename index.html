Why I'm doing this

I wanted to understand the issue, not just talk about it.

Homelessness is something I wanted to learn more about in my own community. Instead of making assumptions, I wanted to research the issue, listen to people who work with it directly, and understand the different factors that can lead to someone losing stable housing.

This project is my way of continuing to learn and sharing what I find with other people. As it grows, I also want to be able to show what the project is actually reaching and accomplishing.

— Wyatt Majors

The goal

Learn first. Listen to people with experience. Share reliable information. Then turn that learning into measurable action.

About the project

Learning first. Acting second.

The goal isn't simply to talk about homelessness. The project is designed to learn about its causes, understand different perspectives, and find ways to make the work measurable.

01

Learn

Research homelessness using reliable sources and learn about the different factors that can lead to housing instability.

02

Listen

Hear from people and organizations that have direct experience working with homelessness and housing services in the community.

03

Measure

Track the project's reach and progress instead of relying only on how many people visit the website.
