01 / WHY THIS PROJECT

One issue.
A lot of people.

Homelessness is often talked about as a statistic. This project is about remembering the people behind those numbers.

I started this project because I wanted to understand homelessness beyond headlines and learn more about what people in my own community experience.

Through volunteering with Helpline House, I have had an opportunity to see how important community support can be. This website is my way of learning more, sharing what I find, and connecting people with reliable information and legitimate resources.

Project goal: Learn, share reliable information, connect people with legitimate community resources, and encourage meaningful action.

02 / THE BIGGER PICTURE

Homelessness
around the world.

There is no single reason someone becomes homeless, and there is no single solution.

Housing costs, job loss, family conflict, health challenges, eviction, and limited access to support can overlap in different ways. Understanding homelessness means looking at both individual circumstances and the larger systems surrounding housing.

LOCAL RESEARCH

What does homelessness look like here?

Bainbridge Island is part of Kitsap County, so county-level data provides one of the clearest official pictures of homelessness affecting the area.

In Kitsap County's 2024 Point-in-Time Count, 597 people were counted as experiencing homelessness on the night of the survey. Of those, 342 people were in sheltered situations and 255 were unsheltered.

The county's survey found that people experiencing unsheltered homelessness were encountered throughout Kitsap County, including Bainbridge Island. Six percent of the unsheltered households surveyed were located on Bainbridge Island.

Importantly, this does not mean only that number of people experience homelessness on Bainbridge. The Point-in-Time Count is a one-night snapshot and the county describes it as an undercount of the actual number of people experiencing homelessness.

KITSAP COUNTY DATA

2024 Point-in-Time Count

597 people experiencing homelessness counted across Kitsap County
255 people counted as unsheltered
342 people counted in sheltered situations

These numbers represent a single-night count, not everyone who experienced homelessness during the entire year.

HOUSING AFFORDABILITY

Housing costs matter.

The City of Bainbridge Island identifies affordable housing as a critical community need. Its Housing Action Plan was created as a long-term strategy to address the island's housing affordability challenges.

The city reports that the average home price on Bainbridge Island exceeds $1.4 million. City information also states that 79% of workers surveyed who do not live on Bainbridge Island said they would live on the island if they could afford to.

These numbers help show why homelessness should not only be understood as someone sleeping outside. Housing insecurity can also involve people struggling to afford rent, facing eviction, living temporarily with others, or having to leave a community because housing is no longer affordable.

WHAT THE KITSAP DATA SHOWS

Among unsheltered households surveyed in 2024 who answered the question about causes, the most common reported factors included health or mental-health issues, housing affordability, job loss or inability to work, and eviction or loss of housing.

This reinforces an important point: homelessness is usually not explained by one simple cause.

LOCAL RESOURCE

Helpline House

Helpline House is a Bainbridge Island organization that connects people with food, assistance, referrals, and other community resources.

The organization reports that more than 350 households shop at its food bank weekly, showing how local support organizations can play an important role in helping community members meet basic needs.
